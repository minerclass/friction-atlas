Station 01 · Chapter One, the problem

The Artifact Test

Unproductive success

School assessment rests on an inference: the essay, lab report, or discussion post is a trace of reading, drafting, synthesis, revision, and understanding. Read the submission below and decide what it still proves.

On the causes of the Dust Bowl

The Dust Bowl of the 1930s resulted from the convergence of ecological vulnerability and economic incentive. Federal land policy encouraged the plowing of shortgrass prairie whose root systems had anchored topsoil for millennia, and wheat prices during the First World War rewarded expansion onto marginal land.

When drought arrived, the exposed soil had nothing holding it. The catastrophe is therefore better understood as a policy failure with an ecological expression than as a natural disaster that happened to strike farmers.

    Station 02 · Chapter Two, media ecology

    The Long Arc

    Ong's developmental account, extended twice

    Ong described successive transformations of the noetic world — the processes of knowing themselves, not a chronology of tools. Three conditions hold tacitly across his account. Visit all five stages and watch what happens to them.

    Condition holds Condition strained Condition no longer holds

    The three conditions are reconstructed in Chapter Two, not quoted. Ong did not present them as a formal list. They are named because they clarify where generative AI places pressure on his account: if they continue to hold, contemporary digital communication is an intensification of secondary orality; if they no longer hold, an extension is warranted.

      The Rupture

      Tertiary algorithmicity and its three pressures

      Each condition that broke at station 02 names a pressure. The three are operationally intertwined but analytically distinct. Assign each condition to the pressure that describes its collapse.

      Select a broken condition, then the pressure it names.

      A note on agentic AI. Agentic systems plan and carry out multistep tasks with reduced human prompting. The draft treats this as an intensification within tertiary algorithmicity rather than a fourth stage, because the defining feature — the algorithmic origination of symbolic content — now extends from producing the artifact to performing the work the artifact once represented.

        Station 04 · Chapter Two, the framework

        The Response

        The Pedagogical Friction Framework

        Four dimensions, but not four peers. Try to build the learner-facing layers first and the atlas will stop you — because that is the framework's central structural claim, and the study treats it as a question to be examined rather than a finding to assume.

        Pedagogical friction is the intentional preservation of educationally productive resistance necessary for durable learning under conditions where frictionless automation has become increasingly available.

        The term is chosen as a counter to its ordinary meaning. In physics, friction is energy lost, to be minimized. The educational argument runs the other way, treating the relevant friction not as wasted energy but as the effort through which cognitive development occurs.
